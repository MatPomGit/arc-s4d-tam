# Project assumptions

These assumptions must be explicit when they affect interpretation.

1. **Normalized contracts are evaluation interfaces, not proof of algorithm equivalence.**
2. **Sensor compatibility is required for fair comparison.** Unequal sensing, calibration, map priors or privileged information must be disclosed.
3. **Pinned software is specification, not evidence.** A baseline becomes evidence only after execution on a frozen compatible cohort.
4. **Sequence/mission is the primary inferential unit.** Frames and tokens are dependent observations.
5. **Reference maps require provenance.** Locked test truth must not be converted into ordinary deployment input.
6. **Synthetic, SIL, HIL and controlled physical validation are different evidence classes.**
7. **Software correctness does not establish scientific superiority or operational safety.**
8. **The confirmatory freeze may fail before required evidence exists.** Missing metadata must never be invented to make it pass.
9. **Missing ground truth produces an unavailable metric, not a zero-error result.**
10. **Scientific conclusions are conditional on the frozen population of datasets/scenarios actually evaluated.**
