# Authorship and contribution policy

Authorship and contribution statements must reflect actual work, not anticipated work.

## CRediT roles

Use the CRediT taxonomy when preparing a manuscript:

- Conceptualization
- Methodology
- Software
- Validation
- Formal analysis
- Investigation
- Data curation
- Writing – original draft
- Writing – review & editing
- Visualization
- Supervision
- Project administration
- Funding acquisition

Do not pre-assign roles that have not been performed. Update the contribution record before submission and whenever authorship changes materially.

## Authorship criteria

An author should make a substantive scholarly contribution, participate in drafting or critical revision, approve the submitted version and accept accountability for their contribution. Contributors who do not meet authorship criteria should be acknowledged according to venue policy.

## AI assistance

AI tools are not authors. Any disclosure of AI-assisted drafting, coding or analysis must follow the current target-venue policy and must not transfer accountability away from the human authors.

## Canonical metadata

The manuscript source is authoritative for final author names and affiliations. `CITATION.cff` must be updated when preferred citation metadata changes for a release.
