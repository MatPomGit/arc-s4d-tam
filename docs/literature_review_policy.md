# Literature review and citation policy

## Search recording

For systematic or publication-facing searches, record in `research/literature_search_log.yaml` the database/index, exact query, search date, filters/date range, screening counts and inclusion/exclusion rationale.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer primary peer-reviewed research for empirical claims, standards for normative requirements, and official dataset/software documentation for implementation facts. Reviews may support synthesis but should not replace primary evidence when the primary study is central to a claim.

## Verification

Before adding a publication-facing citation, verify title, authors, year, venue and DOI/identifier against a reliable source. A citation must support the exact claim it accompanies.

## Recency

For fast-moving technical topics, run a current search near submission. Distinguish established background literature from recent preprints or unreplicated claims.

## Contradictory evidence

Do not omit credible contradictory or null evidence solely because it weakens the proposed narrative. Explain evidentiary differences rather than forcing apparent consensus.
