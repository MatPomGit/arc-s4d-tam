# Terminology glossary

**AlgorithmResult**: normalized output contract evaluated by common metrics.

**Baseline specification**: pinned source revision, container and configuration for an external method. It is not execution evidence.

**Confirmatory analysis**: analysis governed by a frozen prospective protocol before outcome inspection.

**Controlled physical validation**: hardware evaluation performed under a separate safety procedure; it is not equivalent to certification.

**Evidence bundle**: immutable package of outputs plus source/config/data/environment provenance and checksums.

**External comparison**: complete-system comparison of S4D-TAM against other navigation methods under a declared sensing and compute contract.

**Exploratory analysis**: analysis not covered by the applicable prospective confirmatory rule.

**HIL**: hardware-in-the-loop validation preserving relevant hardware timing and interfaces.

**Internal ablation**: full S4D-TAM versus one preregistered H1-H7 mechanism-disabled variant.

**Reference map**: prior spatial representation supplied under a declared provenance and information contract.

**S4D token**: persistent scene/map representation carrying spatial, temporal and semantic state in the S4D-TAM concept.

**SIL**: software-in-the-loop validation in a software-controlled environment.

**Token lifecycle**: creation, association, update and retirement logic for persistent tokens.

**Validation**: evidence that a stated criterion was met in a stated evidence class. Avoid using the word without naming the level and criterion.
